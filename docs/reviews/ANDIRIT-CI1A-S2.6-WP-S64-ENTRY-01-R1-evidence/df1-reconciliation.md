# DF-1 obligation reconciliation

## Original obligation

DF-1 is the full D3 legacy-graph non-interference obligation carried from the
Stage-2 integrated decision into Stage 6. It requires real-fixture evidence that
source-owned occurrence facts remain separate while the same source preserves:

- legacy graph nodes, edges, aggregate `calls`/`references` identities and
  cardinality;
- public query and traversal behavior, including nonempty callers/references;
- native graph reads and raw mirror consumers; and
- exact graph observations across initial publication, replacement, deletion,
  reopen and failed publication.

Synthetic facts, a projected route, or non-mirrored storage cannot discharge it.
The acceptance requires independent reproduction against actual production
storage.
