# S6.4 obligations, evidence and real-fixture corpus

## 1. Governing requirements

The owner selected Candidate C and accepted D3 as an invariant. Source-owned facts belong in dedicated occurrence storage and APIs. The same source must retain legacy callers, references, aggregate `calls`/`references` identities and cardinality, graph nodes/edges, traversal and native consumers. The occurrence side must separately prove correct counts, target-scoped results and consistent semantics across the applicable stores. A compatibility filter that merely hides occurrence graph elements fails the contract. [Owner decision §D.3 and §G](../decisions/adr-ci1-01-owner-decision.md)

The CI1A specification §6.1 requires the smallest existing-corpus combination that covers methods and overloads, calls and references, cross-file and cross-project relationships, contributor union, unit replacement/deletion, graph counts/enumeration and native graph reads. §6.2 and the acceptance matrix §3 require exact graph observations through initial occurrence publication, replacement, deletion, reopen and failed publication. The matrix requires callers/references on memory and native, with native observations for the other D3 rows. Stage 2 acceptance matrix §8 and S2.5 M8.1–M8.4 add real-project published units, real query outputs, unfiltered traversal and raw mirror observations. I-02 requires the canonical corpus to be named. Full D3 was carried as DF-1 in the integrated Stage-2 owner decision and the Stage 6 entry sequence. [Specification](../planning/adr-ci1-01-ci1a-impl-01/specification.md#6-d3--legacy-graph-noninterference) · [CI1A matrix](../planning/adr-ci1-01-ci1a-impl-01/acceptance-matrix.md) · [Stage 2 matrix](../planning/adr-ci1-01-ci1a-impl-01/stage2-acceptance-matrix.md) · [S2.5 matrix](../planning/adr-ci1-01-ci1a-s2-5/wp-s25-4-qualification-matrix.md) · [Obligation register](../decisions/adr-ci1-01-ci1a-s2-obligation-register.md) · [Stage 6 entry](ANDIRIT-CI1A-S2.6-STAGE6-ENTRY-01-obligation-inventory.md)

The D4/D5 dependencies supply truthful publication, failure, reopen and schema boundaries. D4 known precommit failure requires the exact previous accepted graph and facts immediately and after reopen. Unknown outcome retains quarantine and fresh corroboration under the already accepted S6.3 scope. Use known failure for the required D3 rollback case. The earlier Stage 1/S2.5 evidence and each Stage 6 acceptance retain their exact subject and qualifications. Evidence for DF-1 will require independent reproduction against actual production storage.
