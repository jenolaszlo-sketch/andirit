pre-native ThrowIfCancellationRequested), but a THIRD exists in
InMemoryCodeGraphStore.StageIndexUnitAsync (line 311, and again after validation at 347),
driven by MutateAsync:382 before the native transaction opens.

Governance: Contract precision; D4 invariant 5 (outcome taxonomy).

Independent evidence: PROBE H (deterministic, stack trace to
InMemoryCodeGraphStore.cs:311) + source-path citation (MutateAsync:382 drives the
in-memory store before Persist opens the native transaction).

Severity: LOW / non-blocking — the outcome taxonomy and D4 invariant-5 guarantee are
unaffected: it is still "cancelled before native invocation".

Owner disposition: ACCEPT WITH DOCUMENTATION FOLLOW-UP
  The native path correctly documents its two cancellation checkpoints. The third
  managed checkpoint is a documentation-precision finding, not a correctness defect.
  A bounded documentation erratum should clarify: "honoured while waiting for the write
  gate, during the managed projection apply, and immediately before the native
  transaction opens".

Required corrective work: Bounded erratum to C-1/C-2 contract text under a separate
owner-authorized change set. No production change. Not a reason to reopen qualification.

Residual risk: LOW — outcome taxonomy and non-exposure guarantee intact.

operationalization.

Governance: Qualification plan §2 Q-02.

Independent evidence: PROBE A — the Q-02 schedule (concurrent canceller, 20ms delay,
300-node unit) landed in-window in some runs and pre-native in others; NO partial
state in either branch.

Severity: LOW / non-blocking — a justified deviation from the plan's literal
expectation; recorded so the successor does not read it as a gap.

Owner disposition: ACCEPT — PLAN DEVIATION JUSTIFIED
  The producer's invariant framing is the correct operationalization: an untimed
  canceller cannot guarantee which truthful outcome occurs; the assertion must be
  "never partial, never false cancellation" — which holds (PROBE A: 3/3 in-window in
  final run; 0 partial states ever).

Required corrective work: None. Record the deviation in traceability for future
readers.

Residual risk: LOW — no correctness issue; traceability recorded.
