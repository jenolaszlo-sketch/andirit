## 1. Objective

Produce real Roslyn semantic occurrence extraction for the supported `Invocation`
category, mint canonical `asmctx3`/`usage4` identities under the accepted
`S2.2-ID OPTION A ACCEPTED` mapping contract, refuse unsupported/unresolved/
ambiguous/stale/unverified cases with correct coverage, and demonstrate the
result against the frozen, independently accepted S2.0/R2 oracle used as a
bounded reference. No native publication, public API or downstream stage work.

## 5. Required evidence

- Actual inputs (bytes/hash/path/encoding, options, references, exact live
  instances), observed identities and refusal reasons, reproduction commands.
- Frozen expected results taken from the R2 oracle/case matrix; **no expected
  value generated by the extractor under test**.
- Option A §5.2 tests 1–10, adversarial target-binding tests, fabricated-authority
  negatives, coverage/refusal matrix, `compctx2`-isolation guard proof.
- F16 nine-part rows: requirement, authority, production surface, observable
  behavior, positive test, adversary, environment, evidence artifact, closure
  authority.
- Self-excluded manifests, raw logs, TRX, environment; deterministic
  byte-identical repeated/separate-process runs.
- Producer tests and producer runs are **not** independent qualification.

---

## 6. Qualification and acceptance

1. Producer implements WP1–WP8 and records evidence.
2. Independent Q1 review (reviewer not the implementer) reproduces positives,
   negatives and determinism, and verifies the mapping contract.
3. Owner acceptance decision.
4. S2.2 acceptance requires Q-F2 decided, Q-F3 decided before semantic
   acceptance, NEW-04 corrected/independently qualified, live S-01 binding,
   and the mapping review complete.

---

## 7. Stop conditions

Stop the affected work and return to the owner if: `compctx2` cannot be consumed
without changing a frozen identity or entering `usage4`/`asmctx3`; the Option A
durable association cannot be enforced before minting; a defining compilation
cannot be bound to an exact live instance/verified `compctx2`/universe
membership; NEW-01 recovery would be relied upon; D3/D4/D5 would be weakened; a
public API becomes mandatory; Q-F2/Q-F3 cannot be decided; or scope expands into
S2.3+ without separate authorization.

---

## 8. Deliverables of the commission

Source-bound producer evidence, the independently reviewed mapping/compatibility
contract, F16 traceability rows, the independent Q1 review package and the owner
acceptance record. Native publication and downstream stages are explicitly
out of scope.
